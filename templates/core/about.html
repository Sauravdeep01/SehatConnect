About SehatConnect

Bridging the last-mile healthcare access divide in rural India through community empowerment.

Our Core Philosophy

Over 65% of India's population lives in rural villages where public healthcare infrastructure (Sub-Centres, PHCs, and CHCs) exists, but is often difficult to navigate. Lack of smartphones, low digital literacy, and expensive travel to distantly located hospitals lead to overcrowding, late-stage diagnoses, and catastrophic out-of-pocket expenses.

SehatConnect flips the paradigm by empowering accredited community health workers (ASHAs and ANMs). Instead of expecting rural villagers to download apps or navigate portals, our platform equips frontline health workers with digital tools to register patients, reserve OPD appointments with specialist doctors, and arrange life-saving Dial 108 emergency referrals.

Key Operational Standards
  • Zero Digital Burden on Patients: Patients receive simple, tangible printed slips with QR codes verified at hospital OPD counters.
  • Preventing Duplicate Queues: Strict database constraints ensure doctor slots are not overbooked, eliminating hours of uncertain waiting in hospital halls.
  • Dial 108 Transit Integration: Emergency and obstetric referrals alert both the destination hospital and emergency ambulance dispatch simultaneously.
