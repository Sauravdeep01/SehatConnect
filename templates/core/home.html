Role-Based System

Three Tailored Operational Portals

Designed specifically around the rural healthcare workflow without putting any digital burden on patients.

1. Health Worker
ASHA / ANM / MPW
  • Register rural villagers & record vitals
  • Search verified hospitals & doctors
  • Book time-slotted OPD appointments
  • Print bilingual appointment passes
  • Initiate emergency & routine referrals
Health Worker Access
2. Doctor / Staff
Medical Officers & Specialists
  • Live daily OPD patient queue & tokens
  • Accept, reschedule, or complete cases
  • Add clinical diagnosis & prescriptions
  • Triage incoming emergency referrals
  • Update bed availability & services
Doctor / Staff Access
3. Administrator
District CMO / Health Dept
  • Manage hospitals, PHCs, & doctors
  • Verify healthcare facility legitimacy
  • Real-time Chart.js district analytics
  • Monitor appointment & referral flows
  • Onboard & manage health workers
